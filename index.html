PRESSURE CHECK Booster Pump Requirement
Distance exceeds 150m threshold. Significant friction loss across multiple couplings. Auxiliary portable firefighting booster pump required at midpoint to maintain minimum 3.5 bar branchpipe stream.
ALLEY CLEARANCE Evacuation & Hose Route Obstruction
Severe obstruction detected. Dense textile scrap bales and raw material inventory clog the gali corridor, creating hose kink hazards, acute puncture risks, and +90s coupling delay.
FLASHOVER WINDOW Deployment vs Flashover Window
Textile & solvent workshop flashover typically occurs within 5–7 minutes. Estimated hose laying time means water application may arrive near or post flashover. Early alarm is vital.
Delhi Fire Service SOP: Alley Hose Deployment Sequence
1
Tender Positioning: Stage DFS Water Tender at designated arterial road. Chock wheels, engage PTO pump, deploy warning markers.
2
Hose Relay Rollout: Run standard 63mm delivery hoses in dual-crew relay through alleyway. Ensure male instantaneous coupling faces toward fire.
3
Pressure Calibration: Set tender throttle to 6.65 bar. Bleed air at branchpipe before entry into workshop compartment.
4
Branchpipe Attack: Advance attack crew with fog nozzle set to 30° protective cone for thermal radiation shielding at workshop entrance.
